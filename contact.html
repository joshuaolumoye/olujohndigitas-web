Common Questions

Before you reach out — read this.

We know you're busy. Here are the most common questions we get before a project starts.

How quickly can you start on my project?

We typically begin new projects within 3–7 business days of signing off. Rush timelines are available depending on scope and current workload.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely. We have active clients in the UK, Canada, Mauritius, and across Africa, with no drop in quality remotely.

What does your typical process look like?

Discovery → Strategy → Design → Development → Review → Launch → Support, with visibility at every stage.

How do you handle revisions and feedback?

Every project includes revision rounds built into the timeline, with structured feedback loops so nothing gets lost.

Can I get support after the project is launched?

Yes — we offer ongoing maintenance, feature updates, hosting management, and growth support.

Do you also offer training or courses?

Yes — through the OluJohn Academy, we teach Frontend, Backend, and UI/UX to tech aspirants. Visit the Academy page to apply.
