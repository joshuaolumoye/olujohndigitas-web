Common Questions

BEFORE YOU
REACH OUT —
READ THIS.

We know you're busy. Here are the most common questions we get before a project starts — so you can come to us with confidence.

How quickly can you start on my project?

We typically begin new projects within 3–7 business days of signing off. Rush timelines are available depending on scope and current workload — just ask us.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely. We have active clients in the UK, Canada, Mauritius, and across Africa. All project communication and deliverables are handled remotely with no drop in quality.

What does your typical process look like?

Discovery → Strategy → Design → Development → Review → Launch → Support. We give you visibility at every stage and never disappear between milestones.

How do you handle revisions and feedback?

Every project includes revision rounds built into the timeline. We use structured feedback loops so nothing gets lost — and we won't close a milestone until you're genuinely happy with it.

Can I get support after the project is launched?

Yes — we offer ongoing maintenance, feature updates, hosting management, and growth support. Most of our clients stay with us long after launch.
