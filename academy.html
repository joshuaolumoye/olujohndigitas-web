Common Questions

Before you apply.

Do I need prior experience to join?

No. Each track has a beginner-friendly starting point. Let us know your level on the registration form and we'll place you correctly.

Is the Academy free or paid?

There is no tuition fee. Students pay a total of ₦50,000 for the comprehensive course materials and the certificate of completion.

When do classes hold?

Our first class, Web & Mobile App Development, starts on Wednesday, 14 October 2026. Classes hold online every Wednesday and Sunday at 7:00 PM (WAT).

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. The ₦50,000 for course materials and certificate can be paid in instalments. We'll share the payment plan with you once you register.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. The certificate of completion is included in the ₦50,000 fee, along with a portfolio of the projects you build during the program.

Are classes online or onsite?

Classes are fully virtual for now, so you can join from anywhere. We plan to offer onsite classes once we have a physical location, and we'll announce it when that happens.
